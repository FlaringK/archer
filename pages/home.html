About us

Time Archer Studios is dedicated to making the kind of media we want to see in the world without compromising on our core value of keeping the media we create within the hands of the independent creatives we work with. We aim to use our resources to build a community of talented artists and storytellers who want to make animations, audio dramas, and other works that are unapologetically their own while maintaining full creative rights. Keeping it small means that everyone gets to play a part in the process and apply their unique perspectives and expertise in as many ways as they want to contribute via our open floor feedback production style.

Time Archer Studios was co-founded by Melanie Archer (she/he), a trans voice actor and the Director of VOFT Dubs, and Ari Ceppi (they/them) a queer and disabled production specialist and the Showrunner/Executive Producer of Project Soundpage.
