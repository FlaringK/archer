When ghost boy Dante catches Antonio's eye in a book store, he seizes the opportunity to try and gain a confidant and cross-country tour guide after years of being unseen. With the pressure of being the first person in his immigrant family to be afforded the opportunity of higher education weighing on his shoulders, Antonio must decide if he can help Dante with his time-sensitive "unfinished business" AND make it to orientation all on the same day.
